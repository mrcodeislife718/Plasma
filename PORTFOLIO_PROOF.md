# Plasma — Portfolio Proof Contract

**Track:** Interoperability infrastructure

Plasma is complete only when heterogeneous systems can exchange data/actions through explicit contracts with correct translation, failure isolation, observability, and measurable integration value.

Required proof: cross-system integration scenarios; schema/protocol/version compatibility tests; malformed payload, partial failure, retry, ordering, and dependency outage tests; benchmarks for translation/transport latency, throughput, overhead, and error rate; auth/secrets/trust-boundary controls; deployment and real integrations where applicable.

**Next proof target:** connect at least three materially different interfaces through Plasma, inject schema/version and dependency failures, and preserve correctness, recovery, latency, and traceability evidence.
